### 1. [mksglu/context-mode](<https://github.com/mksglu/context-mode>)

*Coding agents · GitHub rank #3 of 12 · TypeScript · License: Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2)*

**What it does:** An MCP server and hooks that keep bulky tool output out of an AI coding agent's context and retrieve session information.
