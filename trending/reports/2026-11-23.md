### 1. [Tracer-Cloud/opensre](<https://github.com/Tracer-Cloud/opensre>)

*Developer tools / AI agents · GitHub rank #13 of 16 · Python · License: Apache-2.0*

**What it does:** A command-line agent for broken CI builds and incidents: OpenSRE **reads the failed run, traces the cause and pushes a fix** for you to review, self-hosted and with your own models.

**Why it is interesting (editorial):** *Material change since the 20 November card: on 22 November it merged **a bridge that turns any MCP server's tools into agent tools**, plus a guided setup command that connects to a server and lists its tools before saving it. **Caveat:** Still **a public alpha**, and the default /auto high level still runs outside writes without asking. A server's read-only flag is declared by the server itself, so the docs say to connect only trusted servers, and product analytics and Sentry error reports are opt-out.

### 2. [zhouxiaoka/autoclip](<https://github.com/zhouxiaoka/autoclip>)

*Video / content tools · GitHub rank #16 of 16 · Python · License: MIT*

**What it does:** A desktop tool that turns long videos into short clips: AutoClip **cuts clips with subtitles, covers and post text** for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Reels, Shorts and other platforms, editing and rendering on your own computer.

**Why it is interesting (editorial):** *Material change since the 20 November card: release v1.5.9, published late on 22 November UTC, reworks failure recovery and publishing and lowers the shortest subtitle highlight from 20 to 10 seconds. Its notes also change diagnostics: when no choice has been saved, **failure diagnostics and a compressed clip of about 60 seconds** from a local video upload automatically, kept for 14 days and not used for training, according to the notes.*

**Caveat:** That **upload default sits badly with the privacy policy**, last updated 9 October, which says videos and audio are never uploaded to the project's servers; check the setting before cutting sensitive footage. The installers are unsigned, and on 23 November the app gained a CometAPI preset with the author's referral link and tracking of referral-link clicks.
